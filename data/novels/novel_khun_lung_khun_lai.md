# 📜 Khun Lung and Khun Lai: The Heavenly Founders of the Tai World
**Tai Khamyang**: *Lik4 Khun1 Lung1 Khun1 Lai1: Pung1 Pu5 Yaa3*
**Genre**: `mythological_epic_novel` | **Informant/Reciter**: Chaw Deben Chowlik
**Location**: Powaimukh, Assam

> Complete unabridged 10-chapter genesis novel of the Tai peoples, narrating the descent from heaven on the golden ladder, the invention of agriculture, the code of Thammathat, and the establishment of Mung Mau.
