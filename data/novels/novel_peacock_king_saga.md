# 📜 The Golden Peacock: The Animal King and the Forest Republic
**Tai Khamyang**: *Pung1 Lik4 Nok4 Yung1 Kham1: Tsaw3 Nok4*
**Genre**: `fable_epic_novel` | **Informant/Reciter**: Chaw Sa Myat Chowlik
**Location**: Powaimukh, Assam

> Complete unabridged 10-chapter moral fable novel detailing the Golden Peacock's morning paritta, the trapper's snares, the philosophical discourse in the King's palace, and the general emancipation of all creatures.
