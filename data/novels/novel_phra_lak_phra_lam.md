# 📜 Phra Lak Phra Lam: The Tai Epic Saga of Prince Rama and Nang Sida
**Tai Khamyang**: *Lik4 Phra2 Lak4 Phra2 Lam1*
**Genre**: `classical_epic_novel` | **Informant/Reciter**: Traditional Manuscript Readers of Powaimukh
**Location**: Powaimukh, Margherita, Assam

> Complete unabridged 14-chapter Tai Khamyang literary epic novel of Phra Lak Phra Lam (The Tai Ramayana), celebrating filial devotion, ascetic trial, the monkey general Hanuman, the causeway of Lanka, and the cosmic triumph of Dhamma.
