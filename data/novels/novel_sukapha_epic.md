# 📜 The Epic of Chau Lung Sukapha: The Great Migration across the Patkai
**Tai Khamyang**: *Pung1 Lik4 Tsaw3 Lung1 Su5 Kaa3 Phaa3*
**Genre**: `historical_epic_novel` | **Informant/Reciter**: Elders of Powaimukh (Chowlik & Thomung lineages)
**Location**: Powaimukh, Margherita, Tinsukia, Assam

> Complete unabridged 12-chapter historical epic novel documenting the 13th-century migration of Prince Chau Lung Sukapha, crossing the Patkai range, settling Powaimukh along the Buridihing (Nam Jin), and establishing eternal harmony with the indigenous peoples of Assam.
