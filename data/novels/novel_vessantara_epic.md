# 📜 The Great Vessantara Saga: The Perfection of Boundless Giving
**Tai Khamyang**: *Lik4 Mahaa1 Wessantara5 Caat4*
**Genre**: `sacred_epic_novel` | **Informant/Reciter**: Chaw Sa Myat Chowlik
**Location**: Powaimukh Monastery (Bapu Chang)

> The complete unabridged 13-khanda Buddhist epic novel of Prince Vessantara, depicting the ultimate perfection of generosity (Dana Parami), ascetic exile on Mount Vamka, the trial of Jujaka, and the triumphant celestial restoration.
