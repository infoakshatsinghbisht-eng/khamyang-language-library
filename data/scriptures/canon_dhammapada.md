# 📜 The Dhammapada: Sacred Verses of the Lord Buddha
**Tai Khamyang**: *Lik4 Dhammapada5: Kham1 Son3 Phra2 Chaw1*
**Genre**: `canonical_scripture` | **Informant/Reciter**: Venerable Sangha of Powaimukh Monastery
**Location**: Powaimukh Monastery (Bapu Chang), Assam

> Complete cover-by-cover canonical verses of the Dhammapada translated into Tai Khamyang with interlinear Lik Tai script, Romanization, Assamese script, and English explanations covering Yamakavagga, Appamadavagga, Chittavagga, Pupphavagga, and Panditavagga.

## Full Canonical Verses & Sentences


### Vagga 1: Yamakavagga (The Twin Verses)

**[dhammapada_v01_s01]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ႁွႃ ၸႆ ပိၼ် ႁွႃ တင် လႆ ႁွႃ ၸႆ ၸႈ တင် လႆ
- **Romanized**: `hua1 tsai1 pin1 hua1 tang1 lai1 hua1 tsai1 tsao3 tang1 lai1`
- **Assamese Script**: হুৱা¹ চাই¹ পিন¹ হুৱা¹ তাঙ¹ লাই¹ হুৱা¹ চাই¹ চ³ তাঙ¹ লাই¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 1: Mind precedes all mental states; mind is their chief; they are mind-wrought."*

**[dhammapada_v01_s02]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ထႃဝ်ႇ ၵုၼ် မိ ႁွႃ ၸႆ ဢမ် ဢ တႃၼ်ႈ ၽႃၼ် ေႁတ်း ၵႃၼ်
- **Romanized**: `thaaw2 kun1 mi1 hua1 tsai1 am1 di1 taan3 phaan1 het4 kaan1`
- **Assamese Script**: থাৱ² কুন¹ মি¹ হুৱা¹ চাই¹ অম¹ অ¹ তান³ ফান¹ হেত্‌⁴ কান¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 1 (cont): If with an impure mind a person speaks or acts, suffering follows him."*

**[dhammapada_v01_s03]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ꩢုၵ်း တူၵ်း ၶႈ မႃ ၽႃၼ် မၼ် ပိၼ် လူတ်း လႃၵ်း ၵႂႃႆ
- **Romanized**: `suk4 tok4 khao3 maa1 phaan1 man1 pin1 lot4 laak4 kwaai1`
- **Assamese Script**: সুক্‌⁴ তোক্‌⁴ খ³ মা¹ ফান¹ মান¹ পিন¹ লোত্‌⁴ লাক্‌⁴ ক্বাই¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 1 (cont): Even as the wheel of the cart follows the hoof of the draft ox."*

**[dhammapada_v01_s04]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ႁွႃ ၸႆ ဢ ꩢႆ တႃၼ်ႈ ၽႃၼ် ေႁတ်း ၵႃၼ် ၸႆႈ
- **Romanized**: `hua1 tsai1 di1 sai1 taan3 phaan1 het4 kaan1 tsai3`
- **Assamese Script**: হুৱা¹ চাই¹ অ¹ সাই¹ তান³ ফান¹ হেত্‌⁴ কান¹ চাই³
- **Translation**: *"Verse 2: If with a pure mind a person speaks or acts with righteousness."*

**[dhammapada_v01_s05]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ꩢုၵ်း ꩢႃင်ႇ မႃ ၽႃၼ် မၼ် ပိၼ် ငႃဝ် ဢမ် ႁႃၵ်း
- **Romanized**: `suk4 saang2 maa1 phaan1 man1 pin1 ngaau1 am1 haak4`
- **Assamese Script**: সুক্‌⁴ সাঙ² মা¹ ফান¹ মান¹ পিন¹ ঙাও¹ অম¹ হাক্‌⁴
- **Translation**: *"Verse 2 (cont): Happiness follows him like his shadow that never departs."*

**[dhammapada_v01_s06]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: မၼ် ဢႈ ႁဝ် မၼ် ၽူၵ်း ႁဝ် မၼ် ေပႇ ႁဝ်
- **Romanized**: `man1 daa3 haw1 man1 phok4 haw1 man1 pe2 haw1`
- **Assamese Script**: মান¹ অ³ হাৱ¹ মান¹ ফোক্‌⁴ হাৱ¹ মান¹ পে² হাৱ¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 3: 'He abused me, he struck me, he defeated me, he robbed me'."*

**[dhammapada_v01_s07]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၵုၼ် ယုႉ မၼ်ႈ ၶမ် ၼႆႊ ဝ ဢမ် ယုတ်း ဝၼ်
- **Romanized**: `kun1 yu5 man3 kham1 nai6 wer1 am1 yut4 wan1`
- **Assamese Script**: কুন¹ যু⁵ মান³ খাম¹ নাই⁶ ৱ¹ অম¹ যুত্‌⁴ ৱান¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 3 (cont): In those who harbor such thoughts, hatred will never cease."*

**[dhammapada_v01_s08]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ဝ ဢမ် ေပႇ ဝ ၼႆႊ မုင် ၼႆႊ ဝၼ် ဝၼ်
- **Romanized**: `wer1 am1 pe2 wer1 nai6 mung1 nai6 wan1 wan1`
- **Assamese Script**: ৱ¹ অম¹ পে² ৱ¹ নাই⁶ মুঙ¹ নাই⁶ ৱান¹ ৱান¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 5: Hatred is never appeased by hatred in this world at any time."*

**[dhammapada_v01_s09]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ဢမ် မိ ဝ ေပႇ ဝ ၼႆႊ ပိၼ် ဢႉ ပုႉ ယႃႈ
- **Romanized**: `am1 mi1 wer1 pe2 wer1 nai6 pin1 dhamma5 pu5 yaa3`

- **Translation**: *"Verse 5 (cont): By non-hatred and love alone is hatred pacified; this is an eternal law."*


### Vagga 2: Appamadavagga (The Verses on Vigilance)

**[dhammapada_v02_s01]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ဢမ် မတ်း ပိၼ် ထႃင် ဢမ် တႆ ၶိင်ႇ ဝၼ်
- **Romanized**: `am1 mat4 pin1 thaang1 am1 tai1 khing2 wan1`
- **Assamese Script**: অম¹ মাত্‌⁴ পিন¹ থাঙ¹ অম¹ তাই¹ খিঙ² ৱান¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 21: Heedfulness (Appamada) is the path to the Deathless (Nibbana)."*

**[dhammapada_v02_s02]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: မတ်း ပိၼ် ထႃင် တႆ ၵုၼ် ဢမ် မတ်း ဢမ် တႆ
- **Romanized**: `mat4 pin1 thaang1 tai1 kun1 am1 mat4 am1 tai1`
- **Assamese Script**: মাত্‌⁴ পিন¹ থাঙ¹ তাই¹ কুন¹ অম¹ মাত্‌⁴ অম¹ তাই¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 21 (cont): Heedlessness is the path to death; the vigilant do not die."*

**[dhammapada_v02_s03]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၵုၼ် မတ်း တင် လႆ ပိၼ် ၵုၼ် တႆ လႈ
- **Romanized**: `kun1 mat4 tang1 lai1 pin1 kun1 tai1 liau3`
- **Assamese Script**: কুন¹ মাত্‌⁴ তাঙ¹ লাই¹ পিন¹ কুন¹ তাই¹ ল³
- **Translation**: *"Verse 21 (cont): While the heedless and reckless are already like unto the dead."*

**[dhammapada_v02_s04]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၽုႉ ပ ယုၵ်း ႁွႃ ၸႆ ꩢုင် ပိၼ် ဢ
- **Romanized**: `phu5 panyaa1 yuk4 hua1 tsai1 sung1 pin1 doi1`
- **Assamese Script**: ফু⁵ প¹ যুক্‌⁴ হুৱা¹ চাই¹ সুঙ¹ পিন¹ অ¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 28: As one standing upon a high rocky mountain peak views those below."*

**[dhammapada_v02_s05]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: မၼ် ႁၼ် ၵုၼ် မတ်း ၼင်ႈ ရွင်း ႁႆႈ ၼႆႊ ထုင်ႇ
- **Romanized**: `man1 han1 kun1 mat4 nang3 rɔng4 hai3 nai6 thung2`
- **Assamese Script**: মান¹ হান¹ কুন¹ মাত্‌⁴ নাঙ³ ৰঅঙ⁴ হাই³ নাই⁶ থুঙ²
- **Translation**: *"Verse 28 (cont): The wise, free from sorrow, looks down upon the grieving multitude."*


### Vagga 3: Chittavagga (The Mind)

**[dhammapada_v03_s01]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ႁွႃ ၸႆ ꩢၼ်ႇ ၽႃၼ် လူမ် ယႃပ်း ၸမ် ၸႆႈ
- **Romanized**: `hua1 tsai1 san2 phaan1 lom1 yaap4 tsam1 tsai3`
- **Assamese Script**: হুৱা¹ চাই¹ সান² ফান¹ লোম¹ যাপ্‌⁴ চাম¹ চাই³
- **Translation**: *"Verse 33: Fickle and fluttering is the mind, difficult to guard and restrain."*

**[dhammapada_v03_s02]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၵုၼ် ပ ꩢူၼ်ႈ ႁွႃ ၸႆ ႁိုႈ ၸူင်
- **Romanized**: `kun1 panyaa1 son3 hua1 tsai1 heu3 tsong1`
- **Assamese Script**: কুন¹ প¹ সোন³ হুৱা¹ চাই¹ হু³ চোঙ¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 33 (cont): The wise man straightens it as an fletcher straightens an arrow shaft."*

**[dhammapada_v03_s03]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ႁွႃ ၸႆ ယုႉ မၼ်ႈ ၽႃၼ် ႁိုႈ ꩢုၵ်း လွႃင်
- **Romanized**: `hua1 tsai1 yu5 man3 phaan1 heu3 suk4 luang1`
- **Assamese Script**: হুৱা¹ চাই¹ যু⁵ মান³ ফান¹ হু³ সুক্‌⁴ লুৱাঙ¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 35: A tamed, guarded, and well-trained mind brings supreme happiness."*


### Vagga 4: Pupphavagga (Flowers)

**[dhammapada_v04_s01]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၵၼ် မွၵ်း ဢမ် ဢၼ် ꩢုႇ လူမ် ၼႆႊ ပႃႉ

- **Translation**: *"Verse 54: The scent of flowers does not travel against the prevailing wind."*

**[dhammapada_v04_s02]**

- **Assamese Script**: কন¹ চন¹ কন¹ অ¹ অম¹ অন¹ সু² লোম¹
- **Translation**: *"Verse 54 (cont): Neither the fragrance of sandalwood, jasmine, nor water lily."*

**[dhammapada_v04_s03]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၵၼ် ၵုၼ် ဢ ဢၼ် ꩢုႇ လူမ် တင် လႆ

- **Translation**: *"Verse 54 (cont): But the fragrance of the virtuous person pervades every quarter of the earth."*


### Vagga 6: Panditavagga (The Wise)

**[dhammapada_v05_s01]**

**[dhammapada_v05_s02]**

**[dhammapada_v05_s03]**

- **Romanized**: `fang1 dhamma5 liau3 hua1 tsai1 sai1 pin1 nong1 nam6`
- **Assamese Script**: ফ়াঙ¹ অ⁵ ল³ হুৱা¹ চাই¹ সাই¹ পিন¹ নোঙ¹ নাম⁶
- **Translation**: *"Verse 82: Having listened to the Dhamma, their hearts become serene and pure as a mountain lake."*
