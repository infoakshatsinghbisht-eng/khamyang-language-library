# 📜 The Maha Paritta Protection Scriptures: Mangala, Ratana, and Metta Suttas
**Tai Khamyang**: *Lik4 Paritta5 Luang1: Mangala5 Ratana5 Metta5*
**Genre**: `canonical_scripture` | **Informant/Reciter**: Chaw Sa Myat Chowlik & Venerable Sangha
**Location**: Powaimukh Monastery (Bapu Chang)

> The foundational protective liturgies (Maha Paritta) chanted daily in Tai Khamyang Buddhist temples for health, peace, harmony, and warding off dangers, fully transcribed sentence by sentence with Lik Tai, Roman, and Assamese scripts.

## Full Canonical Verses & Sentences


### Mangala Sutta: The 38 Auspicious Blessings

**[paritta_s01_v01]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၶိင်ႇ ၼႆႊ ထ မႃ ထုင်ႇ ရိမ် ဝတ်း
- **Romanized**: `khing2 nai6 thewadaa1 maa1 thung2 rim1 wat4`
- **Assamese Script**: খিঙ² নাই⁶ থ¹ মা¹ থুঙ² ৰিম¹ ৱাত্‌⁴
- **Translation**: *"At nightfall, a radiant deity illumined Jetavana and approached the Lord Buddha."*

**[paritta_s01_v02]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: မၼ် ၶွႈ ထႃမ် ဝႃႆႊ ဢၼ် ဢ ပိၼ် မႉ
- **Romanized**: `man1 khɔ3 thaam1 waai6 an1 dai1 pin1 mangala5`
- **Assamese Script**: মান¹ খঅ³ থাম¹ ৱাই⁶ অন¹ অ¹ পিন¹ ম⁵
- **Translation**: *"He inquired: 'Many gods and men yearn for well-being. Tell us of the highest blessing!'."*

**[paritta_s01_v03]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ဢမ် ၶႈ ၵၼ် ၵပ်း ၵုၼ် ၽႃၼ် ပိၼ် မႉ
- **Romanized**: `am1 khao3 kan1 kap4 kun1 phaan1 pin1 mangala5`
- **Assamese Script**: অম¹ খ³ কান¹ কাপ্‌⁴ কুন¹ ফান¹ পিন¹ ম⁵
- **Translation**: *"The Buddha answered: 'Not associating with the foolish, but honoring the wise - this is the highest blessing'."*

**[paritta_s01_v04]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ယုႉ ၼႆႊ ထုင်ႇ ဢ ေႁတ်း ၵႃၼ် ၸႆႈ ပိၼ် မႉ
- **Romanized**: `yu5 nai6 thung2 di1 het4 kaan1 tsai3 pin1 mangala5`
- **Assamese Script**: যু⁵ নাই⁶ থুঙ² অ¹ হেত্‌⁴ কান¹ চাই³ পিন¹ ম⁵
- **Translation**: *"Dwelling in a suitable land, having done merits in the past, setting oneself in the right course - this is the highest blessing."*

**[paritta_s01_v05]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: လျႃင်ႈ ပူ လျႃင်ႈ ေမး ရၵ်း လုၵ်း ပိၼ် မႉ
- **Romanized**: `liang3 po1 liang3 me4 rak4 luk4 pin1 mangala5`
- **Assamese Script**: লিয়াঙ³ পো¹ লিয়াঙ³ মে⁴ ৰাক্‌⁴ লুক্‌⁴ পিন¹ ম⁵
- **Translation**: *"Supporting mother and father, cherishing wife and children, peaceful employment - this is the highest blessing."*

**[paritta_s01_v06]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ဢဝ် ပုၼ်ႈ ၵုၼ် ၽႃၼ် ေႁတ်း ၵႃၼ် ꩢႉ ပိၼ် မႉ
- **Romanized**: `aw1 pun3 kun1 phaan1 het4 kaan1 sila5 pin1 mangala5`
- **Assamese Script**: অৱ¹ পুন³ কুন¹ ফান¹ হেত্‌⁴ কান¹ স⁵ পিন¹ ম⁵
- **Translation**: *"Generosity, righteous conduct, assisting kinsmen, blameless actions - this is the highest blessing."*

**[paritta_s01_v07]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ဢမ် ၵိၼ် လဝ်ႈ ဢမ် မိ မတ်း ပိၼ် မႉ
- **Romanized**: `am1 kin1 lau3 am1 mi1 mat4 pin1 mangala5`
- **Assamese Script**: অম¹ কিন¹ লাও³ অম¹ মি¹ মাত্‌⁴ পিন¹ ম⁵
- **Translation**: *"Abstaining from intoxicants, steadfastness in virtue, heedfulness in Dhamma - this is the highest blessing."*

**[paritta_s01_v08]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: မိ ၶႉ ꩯင် ၶမ် ဢ ပိၼ် မႉ
- **Romanized**: `mi1 khanti5 fang1 kham1 di1 pin1 mangala5`
- **Assamese Script**: মি¹ খ⁵ ফ়াঙ¹ খাম¹ অ¹ পিন¹ ম⁵
- **Translation**: *"Patience, gentleness of speech, seeing the holy monks, timely discussions on Dhamma - this is the highest blessing."*

**[paritta_s01_v09]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ႁွႃ ၸႆ ဢမ် ꩢၼ်ႇ ၽႃၼ် လူၵ်း ပိၼ် မႉ
- **Romanized**: `hua1 tsai1 am1 san2 phaan1 lok4 pin1 mangala5`
- **Assamese Script**: হুৱা¹ চাই¹ অম¹ সান² ফান¹ লোক্‌⁴ পিন¹ ম⁵
- **Translation**: *"A mind unshaken by the eight worldly conditions, sorrowless, stainless, secure - this is the highest blessing."*


### Ratana Sutta: The Jewel Discourse

**[paritta_s02_v01]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၶဝ် ဝႆႈ ၽႇ သဝ် ပိၼ် ရႉ လွႃင်
- **Romanized**: `khaw1 wai3 phra2 chaw1 pin1 ratana5 luang1`
- **Assamese Script**: খাৱ¹ ৱাই³ ফ² ছাৱ¹ পিন¹ ৰ⁵ লুৱাঙ¹
- **Translation**: *"Whatever treasure there is in this world or in the heavens, none equals the Tathagata. In the Buddha is this precious jewel!"*

**[paritta_s02_v02]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၶဝ် ဝႆႈ ဢႉ ပိၼ် ရႉ လွႃင်
- **Romanized**: `khaw1 wai3 dhamma5 pin1 ratana5 luang1`
- **Assamese Script**: খাৱ¹ ৱাই³ অ⁵ পিন¹ ৰ⁵ লুৱাঙ¹
- **Translation**: *"The tranquil, deathless, unconditioned state realized by the Sakyamuni. In the Dhamma is this precious jewel!"*

**[paritta_s02_v03]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၶဝ် ဝႆႈ ꩢႉ ပိၼ် ရႉ လွႃင်
- **Romanized**: `khaw1 wai3 sangha5 pin1 ratana5 luang1`
- **Assamese Script**: খাৱ¹ ৱাই³ স⁵ পিন¹ ৰ⁵ লুৱাঙ¹
- **Translation**: *"The eight noble persons praised by the wise, worthy of gifts. In the Sangha is this precious jewel! By this truth may there be well-being!"*


### Karaniya Metta Sutta: The Hymn of Universal Loving-Kindness

**[paritta_s03_v01]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၵုၼ် တင် လႆ ႁိုႈ မိ မႉ ရိမ် ႁွႃ ၸႆ
- **Romanized**: `kun1 tang1 lai1 heu3 mi1 metta5 rim1 hua1 tsai1`
- **Assamese Script**: কুন¹ তাঙ¹ লাই¹ হু³ মি¹ ম⁵ ৰিম¹ হুৱা¹ চাই¹
- **Translation**: *"One skilled in good, wishing to attain that state of peace, should cultivate boundless goodwill toward all."*

**[paritta_s03_v02]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ပိၼ် ၵုၼ် ꩢႈ ဢမ် မိ ဢမ် ဢတ်း
- **Romanized**: `pin1 kun1 sue3 am1 mi1 am1 at4`
- **Assamese Script**: পিন¹ কুন¹ স³ অম¹ মি¹ অম¹ অত্‌⁴
- **Translation**: *"Let him be able, upright, perfectly upright, compliant, gentle, and free from pride."*

**[paritta_s03_v03]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ပိၼ် ၵုၼ် ဢိမ် ငႆႇ လျႃင်ႈ ငႆႇ ဢမ် မိ ငွၼ်ႉ
- **Romanized**: `pin1 kun1 im1 ngai2 liang3 ngai2 am1 mi1 ngɔn5`
- **Assamese Script**: পিন¹ কুন¹ ইম¹ ঙাই² লিয়াঙ³ ঙাই² অম¹ মি¹ ঙঅন⁵
- **Translation**: *"Contented, easily supported, with few duties, living lightly, with senses calmed."*

**[paritta_s03_v04]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: တူ ꩢတ်း တင် လႆ ႁိုႈ မိ ꩢုၵ်း ယုႉ မၼ်ႈ
- **Romanized**: `to1 sat4 tang1 lai1 heu3 mi1 suk4 yu5 man3`
- **Assamese Script**: তো¹ সাত্‌⁴ তাঙ¹ লাই¹ হু³ মি¹ সুক্‌⁴ যু⁵ মান³
- **Translation**: *"May all beings be happy and secure; may their hearts be wholesome and glad!"*

**[paritta_s03_v05]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ꩢတ်း ဢူၼ်ႈ ꩢတ်း လွႃင် ꩢတ်း ဢမ် ꩢတ်း ၶႃဝ်
- **Romanized**: `sat4 on3 sat4 luang1 sat4 dam1 sat4 khaau1`
- **Assamese Script**: সাত্‌⁴ ওন³ সাত্‌⁴ লুৱাঙ¹ সাত্‌⁴ অম¹ সাত্‌⁴ খাও¹
- **Translation**: *"Whatever living beings there may be: weak or strong, long or tall, medium or small."*

**[paritta_s03_v06]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ပိၼ် ပိၼ် ေမး ရၵ်း လုၵ်း ဢဝ် ၼႆႊ ႁွႃ ၸႆ
- **Romanized**: `pin1 pin1 me4 rak4 luk4 diaw1 nai6 hua1 tsai1`
- **Assamese Script**: পিন¹ পিন¹ মে⁴ ৰাক্‌⁴ লুক্‌⁴ অৱ¹ নাই⁶ হুৱা¹ চাই¹
- **Translation**: *"Even as a mother protects with her life her child, her only child, so with a boundless heart should one cherish all living beings."*
