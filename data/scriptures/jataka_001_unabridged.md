# 📜 Apannaka Jataka: The Incontrovertible Truth and the Water Mirage (Complete Story)
**Tai Khamyang**: *Lik4 Caat4 001: Apannaka5 Caat4 (Unabridged)*
**Genre**: `buddhist_jataka_complete` | **Informant/Reciter**: Chaw Sa Myat Chowlik
**Location**: Powaimukh, Assam

> Complete unabridged edition of the first canonical Jataka, recounting the wise caravan leader who saw through the demon's mirage in the desert sands, contrasted with the foolish merchant.

## Full Canonical Verses & Sentences


### Apannaka Jataka (Unabridged)

**[jataka_001_s01]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၽႇ သဝ် ယုႉ ဝတ်း ေသ တႃ ဝၼ် တႃၼ်ႈ ဢႉ ဢတ်း
- **Romanized**: `phra2 chaw1 yu5 wat4 che1 ta1 wan1 taan3 apannaka5 caat4`
- **Assamese Script**: ফ² ছাৱ¹ যু⁵ ৱাত্‌⁴ ছে¹ তা¹ ৱান¹ তান³ অ⁵ অত্‌⁴
- **Translation**: *"The Lord Buddha dwelt at Jetavana and related the Apannaka Jataka to the five hundred merchants."*

**[jataka_001_s02]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၼႆႊ မုင် ဢႉ မိ ၽုႉ ပ ၵပ်း ၽုႉ ၽႃၼ်
- **Romanized**: `nai6 mung1 banaras5 mi1 phu5 panyaa1 kap4 phu5 phaan1`
- **Assamese Script**: নাই⁶ মুঙ¹ অ⁵ মি¹ ফু⁵ প¹ কাপ্‌⁴ ফু⁵ ফান¹
- **Translation**: *"In the city of Varanasi, there lived two caravan merchants: one wise and thoughtful, the other foolish and greedy."*

**[jataka_001_s03]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၶဝ် တင်ႈ လူတ်း ႁႃႈ ရူႆး တူႇ ပႆ ထႃင် ꩢႃႆ
- **Romanized**: `khaw1 tang3 lot4 haa3 roi4 to2 pai1 thaang1 saai1`
- **Assamese Script**: খাৱ¹ তাঙ³ লোত্‌⁴ হা³ ৰওই⁴ তো² পাই¹ থাঙ¹ সাই¹
- **Translation**: *"Each prepared five hundred bullock carts laden with silks and spices to cross the great arid sandy desert."*

**[jataka_001_s04]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၽုႉ ၽႃၼ် ပႆ ၵူၼ်ႇ ၼႆႊ ထႃင် ၽႃၼ်ႇ ယႃပ်း
- **Romanized**: `phu5 phaan1 pai1 kon2 nai6 thaang1 phaan2 yaap4`
- **Assamese Script**: ফু⁵ ফান¹ পাই¹ কোন² নাই⁶ থাঙ¹ ফান² যাপ্‌⁴
- **Translation**: *"The foolish merchant set off first, seeking to outrun his rival and reap the highest market profit."*

**[jataka_001_s05]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ယၵ်း လွႃင် ၽႃၼ် ပိၼ် ၵုၼ် မႃင်ႇ လူတ်း ၼမ်ႊ
- **Romanized**: `yak4 luang1 phaan1 pin1 kun1 maang2 lot4 nam6`
- **Assamese Script**: যাক্‌⁴ লুৱাঙ¹ ফান¹ পিন¹ কুন¹ মাঙ² লোত্‌⁴ নাম⁶
- **Translation**: *"In the desert sands, an ogre transformed into a radiant prince drenched in wet lotus garlands, riding a cart with dripping wheels."*

**[jataka_001_s06]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: မၼ် ဝႃႆႊ ၼႃႈ မိ ꩯူၼ် ၼမ်ႊ လႆ ေတမ်
- **Romanized**: `man1 waai6 naa3 mi1 fon1 nam6 lai1 tem1`
- **Assamese Script**: মান¹ ৱাই⁶ না³ মি¹ ফ়োন¹ নাম⁶ লাই¹ তেম¹
- **Translation**: *"The demon deceitfully claimed: 'Ahead there are torrential rains, emerald pastures, and rushing streams; cast away your heavy water jars!'."*

**[jataka_001_s07]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၽုႉ ၽႃၼ် ꩯင် ၶမ် ယၵ်း ဢဝ် ၼမ်ႊ ေထႉ ၽႃၼ်
- **Romanized**: `phu5 phaan1 fang1 kham1 yak4 aw1 nam6 the5 phaan1`
- **Assamese Script**: ফু⁵ ফান¹ ফ়াঙ¹ খাম¹ যাক্‌⁴ অৱ¹ নাম⁶ থে⁵ ফান¹
- **Translation**: *"The gullible, foolish merchant believed the demon's lies and emptied all his heavy water vessels into the burning sands."*

**[jataka_001_s08]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၶဝ် ပႆ ထုင်ႇ ဢမ် မိ ၼမ်ႊ ၵုၼ် တင် လႆ တႆ
- **Romanized**: `khaw1 pai1 thung2 am1 mi1 nam6 kun1 tang1 lai1 tai1`
- **Assamese Script**: খাৱ¹ পাই¹ থুঙ² অম¹ মি¹ নাম⁶ কুন¹ তাঙ¹ লাই¹ তাই¹
- **Translation**: *"They traveled onward through the scorching dunes, found no water, collapsed from dehydration, and were devoured by the ogres."*

**[jataka_001_s09]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၽုႉ ပ မႃ ထုင်ႇ ႁၼ် ယၵ်း မႃင်ႇ လူတ်း
- **Romanized**: `phu5 panyaa1 maa1 thung2 han1 yak4 maang2 lot4`
- **Assamese Script**: ফু⁵ প¹ মা¹ থুঙ² হান¹ যাক্‌⁴ মাঙ² লোত্‌⁴
- **Translation**: *"Later, the wise merchant arrived and immediately discerned the deception of the ogre from the unnatural, red, unwinking eyes."*

**[jataka_001_s10]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: မၼ် ꩢူၼ်ႈ ၵုၼ် ဢမ် ႁိုႈ ေထႉ ၼမ်ႊ ꩢႆ
- **Romanized**: `man1 son3 kun1 am1 heu3 the5 nam6 sai1`
- **Assamese Script**: মান¹ সোন³ কুন¹ অম¹ হু³ থে⁵ নাম⁶ সাই¹
- **Translation**: *"He commanded his men: 'Do not pour out a single drop of water until you see the true river with your own eyes!'."*

**[jataka_001_s11]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၶဝ် ၶႃႈ ထႃင် ꩢႃႆ ꩢုၵ်း ထုင်ႇ မုင် မႆႇ
- **Romanized**: `khaw1 khaa3 thaang1 saai1 suk4 thung2 mung1 mai2`
- **Assamese Script**: খাৱ¹ খা³ থাঙ¹ সাই¹ সুক্‌⁴ থুঙ² মুঙ¹ মাই²
- **Translation**: *"They safely crossed the perilous desert, sold their merchandise at immense profit, and returned home rejoicing."*

**[jataka_001_s12]**
- **Lik Tai Script**: ၽႇ သဝ် ဝႃႆႊ ၽုႉ ပ ၶိင်ႇ ၼၼ်ႊ ပိၼ် ႁဝ်
- **Romanized**: `phra2 chaw1 waai6 phu5 panyaa1 khing2 nan6 pin1 haw1`
- **Assamese Script**: ফ² ছাৱ¹ ৱাই⁶ ফু⁵ প¹ খিঙ² নান⁶ পিন¹ হাৱ¹
- **Translation**: *"The Buddha concluded: 'The wise leader of old was I myself. Cultivate unwavering discernment!'."*
