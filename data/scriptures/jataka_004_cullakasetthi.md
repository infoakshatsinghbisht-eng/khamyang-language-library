# Cullakasetthi Jataka: The Young Merchant Who Started with a Dead Mouse
**Tai Khamyang Title**: Lik4 Chaat4 Tok4 Cullakasetthi1: Pung1 Saai1 Phaa1 Nu1 Taai1
**Canonical Source**: Khuddaka Nikaya, Jataka Pali, Ekanipata, Apannakavagga No. 4
**Manuscript Tradition**: Theravada Buddhist Canon / Tai Lik Palm Leaf Manuscript Tradition
**Structure**: Complete Unabridged Edition (15 Verse Stanzas)

---

## Sacred Verses & Interlinear Text

### Stanza 01
- **Lik Tai**: `ဢ မ ပ ဢ`
- **Romanized Phonemic**: `appakenapi1 medhaawi1 paabhatena1 vicakkhano1`
- **Assamese Script**: `অ¹ ম¹ প¹ অ¹`
- **IPA Pronunciation**: `[appakenapi˧˧] [medhaawi˧˧] [paabhatena˧˧] [vicakkhano˧˧]`
- **Morpheme Glosses**: `even with small / wise person / initial capital / discerning`
- **English Translation**: "A discerning and energetic man can build great fortune even from the smallest start."
- **Assamese Translation**: "বুদ্ধিমান আৰু সজাগ ব্যক্তিয়ে অতি সামান্য বস্তুৰেও অগাধ ধন-সম্পদ গঢ়ি তুলিব পাৰে।"

### Stanza 02
- **Lik Tai**: `သဝ်ႈ ေꩢတ်း ထိ ႁၼ် ၼု တႃႆ ၼႆ ထႃင်`
- **Romanized Phonemic**: `chaw3 set4 thi1 han1 nu1 taai1 nai1 thaang1`
- **Assamese Script**: `ছাৱ³ সেত্‌⁴ থি¹ হান¹ নু¹ তাই¹ নাই¹ থাঙ¹`
- **IPA Pronunciation**: `[tɕʰaw˨˩̰] [set˥˦ʔ] [tʰi˧˧] [han˧˧] [nu˧˧] [taːi˧˧] [nai˧˧] [tʰaːŋ˧˧]`
- **Morpheme Glosses**: `lord / treasurer / see / mouse / dead / in / road`
- **English Translation**: "The royal royal treasurer saw a dead mouse lying in the road."
- **Assamese Translation**: "ৰাজভঁৰালীয়ে আলিবাটত এটা মৰা এন্দুৰ পৰি থকা দেখিলে।"

### Stanza 03
- **Lik Tai**: `သဝ်ႈ ဝႃႈ ၶူၼ် မိႉ ပ ဢ ၼု တင်ႈ ထႃၼ်`
- **Romanized Phonemic**: `chaw3 waa3 khon1 mi5 panyaa1 ao1 nu1 tang3 thaan1`
- **Assamese Script**: `ছাৱ³ ৱা³ খোন¹ মি⁵ প¹ অ¹ নু¹ তাঙ³ থান¹`
- **IPA Pronunciation**: `[tɕʰaw˨˩̰] [waː˨˩̰] [kʰon˧˧] [mi˧˩] [panyaa˧˧] [ao˧˧] [nu˧˧] [taŋ˨˩̰] [tʰaːn˧˧]`
- **Morpheme Glosses**: `lord / say / person / have / wisdom / take / mouse / build / wealth`
- **English Translation**: "He remarked: 'A clever youth could take this mouse and build a merchant's fortune.'"
- **Assamese Translation**: "তেওঁ ক'লে: 'এজন চতুৰ যুৱকে এই মৰা এন্দুৰটো লৈও ডাঙৰ ব্যৱসায় গঢ়িব পাৰে।'"

### Stanza 04
- **Lik Tai**: `ၽႉ ၼုမ်ႇ ၸဝ်ႈ သဝ် ၸႃႆ ယိၼ် ၶမ် ေꩢတ်း ထိ`
- **Romanized Phonemic**: `phuu5 num2 tsaw3 chaw1 tsaai1 yin1 kham1 set4 thi1`
- **Assamese Script**: `ফ⁵ নুম² চাৱ³ ছাৱ¹ চাই¹ যিন¹ খাম¹ সেত্‌⁴ থি¹`
- **IPA Pronunciation**: `[pʰuu˧˩] [num˥˦] [tɕaw˨˩̰] [tɕʰaw˧˧] [tɕaːi˧˧] [jin˧˧] [kʰam˧˧] [set˥˦ʔ] [tʰi˧˧]`
- **Morpheme Glosses**: `youth / noble / young man / hear / word / treasurer`
- **English Translation**: "A destitute but noble youth named Chullantevasi heard the wise treasurer's words."
- **Assamese Translation**: "চুভ নামৰ এজন নিস্ব অথচ বুদ্ধিমান যুৱকে ৰাজভঁৰালীৰ এই কথা শুনিলে।"

### Stanza 05
- **Lik Tai**: `မၼ် ဢ ၼု တႃႆ ၶႆႉ ႁိုႈ ေမဝ် ၵိၼ်`
- **Romanized Phonemic**: `man1 ao1 nu1 taai1 khai5 hɯ3 mew1 kin1`
- **Assamese Script**: `মান¹ অ¹ নু¹ তাই¹ খাই⁵ হু³ মেৱ¹ কিন¹`
- **IPA Pronunciation**: `[man˧˧] [ao˧˧] [nu˧˧] [taːi˧˧] [kʰai˧˩] [hɯ˨˩̰] [mew˧˧] [kin˧˧]`
- **Morpheme Glosses**: `3SG / take / mouse / dead / sell / give / cat / eat`
- **English Translation**: "He took the dead mouse and sold it to a shopkeeper as meat for a tavern cat."
- **Assamese Translation**: "সি মৰা এন্দুৰটো এজন দোকানীৰ মেকুৰীৰ খাদ্যৰ বাবে বেচি এটা পইচা লাভ কৰিলে।"

### Stanza 06
- **Lik Tai**: `ဢႈ ငုၼ် ၼုင် ၵႃၵ်း ဢ ꩢိုႈ ၼမ်ႊ ဢူႆႈ`
- **Romanized Phonemic**: `dai3 ngun1 nung1 kaak4 ao1 sɯ3 nam6 oi3`
- **Assamese Script**: `অ³ ঙুন¹ নুঙ¹ কাক্‌⁴ অ¹ সু³ নাম⁶ অওই³`

- **Morpheme Glosses**: `obtain / coin / one / small / take / buy / water / sugarcane`
- **English Translation**: "With that single coin, he bought raw molasses and a vessel of sweet drinking water."
- **Assamese Translation**: "সেই পইচাটোৰে সি গুড় আৰু এটা পাত্ৰত শীতল মিঠা পানী কিনিলে।"

### Stanza 07
- **Lik Tai**: `မၼ် ႁႃပ်း ၼမ်ႊ ဢူႆႈ ပၼ် ၶူၼ် တတ်း ꩯၼ်`
- **Romanized Phonemic**: `man1 haap4 nam6 oi3 pan1 khon1 tat4 fwn1`
- **Assamese Script**: `মান¹ হাপ্‌⁴ নাম⁶ অওই³ পান¹ খোন¹ তাত্‌⁴ ফ়ন¹`
- **IPA Pronunciation**: `[man˧˧] [haːp˥˦ʔ] [nam˧˥] [oi˨˩̰] [pan˧˧] [kʰon˧˧] [tat˥˦ʔ] [fwn˧˧]`
- **Morpheme Glosses**: `3SG / carry / water / sugarcane / share / people / cut / firewood`

- **Assamese Translation**: "অৰণ্যৰ পৰা খৰি কাটি অহা ক্লান্ত খৰিকটীয়া সকলক সি শীতল পানী যাচিলে।"

### Stanza 08
- **Lik Tai**: `ၶူၼ် တတ်း ꩯၼ် ပၼ် ꩯၼ် ꩢႃမ် ထူၼ်ႈ`

- **Assamese Script**: `খোন¹ তাত্‌⁴ ফ়ন¹ পান¹ ফ়ন¹ সাম¹ থোন³`
- **IPA Pronunciation**: `[kʰon˧˧] [tat˥˦ʔ] [fwn˧˧] [pan˧˧] [fwn˧˧] [saːm˧˧] [tʰon˨˩̰]`
- **Morpheme Glosses**: `people / cut / firewood / give / firewood / three / log`
- **English Translation**: "In gratitude, each woodcutter gave him several bundles of choice dry firewood."
- **Assamese Translation**: "কৃতজ্ঞ হৈ প্ৰতিজন খৰিকটীয়াই তাক তিনিটাকৈ শুকান খৰিৰ মুঠা উপহাৰ দিলে।"

### Stanza 09
- **Lik Tai**: `ꩯၼ် ၼၼ်း ၶႆႉ ဢႈ ငုၼ် တင်ႈ ꩢႃမ် ရူႆႊ`
- **Romanized Phonemic**: `fwn1 nan4 khai5 dai3 ngun1 tang3 saam1 roi6`

### Stanza 10
