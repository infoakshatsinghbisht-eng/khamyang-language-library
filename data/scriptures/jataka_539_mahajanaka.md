# Mahajanaka Jataka: The Great King Who Swam the Ocean of Boundless Faith
**Tai Khamyang Title**: Lik4 Chaat4 Tok4 Mahaajanaka1: Chaw3 Phaa4 Waai3 Nam6 Pin1 Thaan1
**Canonical Source**: Khuddaka Nikaya, Jataka Pali, Mahanipata Jataka No. 539
**Manuscript Tradition**: Theravada Buddhist Canon / Tai Lik Palm Leaf Manuscript Tradition
**Structure**: Complete Unabridged Edition (16 Verse Stanzas)

---

## Sacred Verses & Interlinear Text

### Stanza 01
- **Lik Tai**: `ဢ ပ ယ ဢ ၼ`
- **Romanized Phonemic**: `vaayametheva1 puriso1 yaava1 atthassa1 nipphadaa1`
- **Assamese Script**: `অ¹ প¹ য¹ অ¹ ন¹`
- **IPA Pronunciation**: `[vaayametheva˧˧] [puriso˧˧] [jaava˧˧] [atthassa˧˧] [nipphadaa˧˧]`
- **Morpheme Glosses**: `strive should / a man / as long as / purpose / accomplished`
- **English Translation**: "A righteous person must persevere untiringly as long as the noble goal is not yet achieved."
- **Assamese Translation**: "যতদিন উদ্দেশ্য সিদ্ধ নহয়, ততদিন ধৰ্মপৰায়ণ মানুহে নিৰন্তৰ সাধনা কৰি থকা উচিত।"

- **Assamese Script**: `ছাৱ³ ম¹ চান¹ না¹ কা¹ লোঙ¹ হ¹ খা³`

- **Morpheme Glosses**: `lord / Mahajanaka / board / ship / trade`
