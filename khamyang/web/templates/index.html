Noun Classifier Agreement

Tai syntax requires: Noun + Numeral + Classifier (e.g. maa1 sɔŋ1 to1 = 2 dogs).

maa1 sɔŋ1 to1
Lik Tai: မႃ တူဝ် • Assamese: মা¹ চং¹ টো¹
Classifier: to1 (Living animals, quadrupeds, birds)
